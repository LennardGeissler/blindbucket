# Code of conduct

The rules are short and live in one place, so that they cannot come to say two
different things: [CONTRIBUTING.md, "How we behave here"](CONTRIBUTING.md#how-we-behave-here).

In brief: argue with the design as hard as you like, and not with the person.
Reviews here are direct and ask *why* before *whether*; that is about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning or discriminatory comments, and
publishing anyone's private information are not welcome and are removed;
repeated after a warning, they end participation.

If something here reads as crossing that line, say so in the thread or tell the
maintainer, [@LennardGeissler](https://github.com/LennardGeissler). Anything
that also breaks GitHub's own rules can be reported to GitHub directly at
<https://github.com/contact/report-abuse>, which reaches someone other than the
maintainer of this repository.
